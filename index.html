01 / RESEARCH OVERVIEW
01 / ABOUT

Science driven by curiosity.
Research built for impact.

My research examines how Atlantic salmon cells recognize and respond to viral infection, and how molecular tools can help us investigate antiviral immunity.

My 2025 PhD thesis at the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU), Faculty of Biosciences / CIGENE, brought together promoter evaluation, transcriptomic profiling, and CRISPR-Cas9 receptor knockout studies in salmon head kidney (SHK-1) cells.

The broader goal is to strengthen our understanding of host–pathogen interactions and contribute methods for functional genomics and sustainable aquaculture research.

Using IFNa treatment, viral challenges with ISAV and IPNV, and RNA-seq to identify shared and virus-specific defense responses.

Key finding IFNa induced antiviral genes and reduced viral replication in tested cells.
