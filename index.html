01 / RESEARCH OVERVIEW
01 / ABOUT

Science driven by curiosity.
Research built for impact.

My research examines how Atlantic salmon cells recognize and respond to viral infection, and how molecular tools can help us investigate antiviral immunity.

My PhD, awarded in 2025 by the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU), Faculty of Biosciences / CIGENE, brought together promoter evaluation, transcriptomic profiling, and CRISPR-Cas9 receptor knockout studies in salmon head kidney (SHK-1) cells.

The broader goal is to strengthen our understanding of host–pathogen interactions and contribute methods for functional genomics and sustainable aquaculture research.
